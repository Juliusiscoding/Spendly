Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here and in our Terms and Conditions.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the information needed to run the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you choose to record.
  • Basic technical data — such as your browser type and log-in times, used to keep the service secure and working.

We do not ask for, and you should not enter, bank account numbers, card details, or other sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store your expenses and show you summaries, category breakdowns, and filtered views.
  • Keep the service secure, prevent misuse, and fix problems.
  • Send you important notices about your account or changes to our policies.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our database and protected with reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including password hashing and restricted access. No system is completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal and expense data within a reasonable period, except where we must keep it to meet legal obligations.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on a small number of trusted providers to operate, such as hosting services and web font providers. These providers only receive the information needed to perform their function and are expected to protect it.

We do not share your expense data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users and the service.

5. Your Rights

You can view and edit your expense data at any time from your account. You may also request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account and associated information.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Spendly team. We will do our best to respond promptly.
